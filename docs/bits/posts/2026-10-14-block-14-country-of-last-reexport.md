Don't talk to the APHIS guy with the mustache.* I didn't ask why. Brazil. Pre-ban. 1988 harvest, per the chain of custody: Fazenda Santa Luzia → São Paulo broker → Miami importer → Baltimore warehouse → Elias's basement → this case. Each stamp a signature. He didn't offer help. He checked his phone.

Block 5: Species
Dalbergia nigra. Appendix I. The color card is a lie we all agree on. The heartwood in the case is darker, redder, alive in a way the laminate isn't. He uses it like a jeweler. He once made a broker cry over a smudged ink stamp.

Aris sent a fruit basket to the ICU. No card.

The snipe is real. Elias's planer has a nicked blade. He refuses to replace it. Says it gives character.
